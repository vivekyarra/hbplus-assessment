HB+ Workspace

Privacy policy

HB+ Workspace is an assessment application that restricts access to verified Google accounts with an @hbplus.fit email address.

Information used

When you choose Google Sign-In, Google sends Supabase your account identifier, email address, email verification status, and basic profile information. Supabase uses that information to authenticate you. The app uses your verified email to decide whether you can access the dashboard. Google accounts outside the allowed domain are denied before a workspace profile is created.

Storage and sharing

Supabase stores the account identity and the workspace profile for eligible users. The workspace profile contains the account identifier, role, and date created. Vercel hosts the web app. The app does not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, or other Google services, and does not sell Google user data. The browser keeps the session in tab-scoped storage. The mobile app keeps its session in device secure storage.

Retention and deletion

Signing out removes the app’s local session. If you want the operator to delete your account and workspace profile, contact vivekyarra567@gmail.com.
